# Changes — 30 September to 5 October 2026

What was built and fixed since the 30 September session, what each change needs
in order to work, and what is still open. Branch: `feature/maps-categories-gallery`.

**Verification status.** Typecheck, lint (0 errors, 27 pre-existing warnings)
and the 39 unit tests pass. The visits, geocoding and catalog code was also run
against the real database. The Leaflet map was rendered in a browser. Nothing
below has been run end to end on a phone; treat it as built but untested on
devices.

---

## 1. Store location and maps

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Business page | A **Location** section (with its own tab) showing an interactive map, the address, a full-screen view and a Google Maps directions button |
| Onboarding step 2 | Merchants tap the map or drag the pin onto their store; "Use my current location" drops it where they stand |
| Backend | Addresses saved without a pin are looked up in the background (OpenStreetMap Nominatim) and stored as an *approximate* location |

- **Map engine:** Leaflet 1.9.4 inside `react-native-webview`, over OpenStreetMap
  tiles. No API key on Android or iOS. Leaflet is loaded from unpkg with
  subresource-integrity hashes (`src/components/LeafletMap.tsx`).
- **Fallback:** builds without the WebView native module, and the web build, use
  `src/components/TileMap.tsx`, a plain-JavaScript map (drag, +/− zoom, no pinch).
  Native modules are loaded defensively, so older app binaries do not crash.
- **Location precision** (`businesses.location_precision`, migration 033):
  `pin` (placed by the merchant), `address` (looked up, shown with a shaded
  circle and the note "Approximate location") or `none` (the Nairobi placeholder,
  never shown on a map).
- **Backfill:** `npm run geocode` in `backend/` (add `-- --dry-run` to preview).
  Already run: 6 of 7 businesses located; Sarah Beauty Haven's address is only
  "Nairobi".
- `react-native-maps` was tried and removed: without a Google key its Android
  map renders black.

## 2. Categories

- Merchants pick **up to 5 categories** in onboarding step 1; the first is the
  main one (`businesses.category_ids`, migration 031).
- A business is also listed under the categories of its active services.
- Search chips, filtering and text search, the home counts and "nearby" all match
  any of a business's categories.
- **Fixed:** the home **Barber** and **Gym** tiles used ids (`barber`, `gym`) no
  merchant is saved under, so they always showed 0. They now use `barbering` and
  `fitness`; old ids are mapped in Search. Fitness uses `assets/icons/gym.png`
  (`weightlifting.png` was deleted).

## 3. Photos

- **Gallery captions:** merchants give each Studio Gallery photo a title and price,
  or fill them from one of their services (migration 032). Customers see the
  caption in the full-screen photo viewer. Service photos are captioned with the
  service name and price automatically.
- **Hero carousel:** the business page hero cycles through the storefront photos
  every 4 seconds, can be swiped, shows position dots and "3 / 10", and respects
  the phone's reduce-motion setting (`src/components/HeroCarousel.tsx`).
- **Home:** the Beauty and Fitness hero cards use `Beauty.webp` and `gym.webp`.
- **Logo upload in onboarding step 1** was a placeholder button with no action.
  It now offers Take Photo / From Library. For a new merchant the photo uploads
  at once and is attached when step 1 is saved (the business does not exist
  before then).

## 4. Search

- Search opens on **all businesses** ("All 6 businesses · 4 bookable") instead of
  an empty recent-searches panel. Recent searches remain as chips above the list.
- Tapping the Search tab resets the query and category to All. Returning from a
  business page keeps the search.

## 5. Merchant Sales

- **Fixed:** the tab worked out "today" once and never again, so after midnight
  (or on the 1st) the daily, weekly and monthly totals showed the old period. It
  now re-reads the date on focus, on return to the foreground and every minute.
- Monthly chart gets a **Wk 5** bar for days 29–31.
- **Delete** a hand-entered transaction (bookings are removed by cancelling them).
  "Show all" lists every entry in the period.
- **Back-dating:** the Add Transaction form has a ‹ Today › stepper, up to 90 days
  back; the server rejects future dates and older ones.
- **Bookings count once they have happened:** completed bookings always; confirmed
  ones once their appointment time has passed. Upcoming confirmed bookings are
  listed in a banner instead of inflating totals
  (`src/merchant/sales-rules.ts`).

## 6. Merchant Store visits card (Profile tab)

Built from `Inspo/StoreVisitsCard.dc.html` and `Inspo/Profile Store Visits.dc.html`.

- Today / Week / Month (calendar periods, week Monday–Sunday): visits, unique
  visitors, a bar per day, contacted / tapped Book / saved, and "X of Y visitors
  got in touch".
- Comparison against the **same stretch** of the previous period (today vs this
  time yesterday, week vs the same days last week, month vs the same days last
  month), shown only when the previous period had visits.
- States: loading, error, no visits yet (with Share your store), not live yet,
  collapsed (remembered on the phone).
- **Counts only:** visitor ids never leave the server. The merchant's own phone
  is excluded.
- **Fixed double counting:** opening a business from a listing card logged the
  visit twice. The card no longer logs it, and the server ignores the old
  duplicates (`source_section = 'provider_card'`).
- Endpoint: `GET /api/merchant/insights/visits?range=&tzOffset=&exclude=`
  (`backend/src/services/merchant-insights.js`, logic in `visit-stats.js`).

## 7. Merchant onboarding hours

Weekly Operating Hours offers three choices: **Mon – Fri**, **7 Days a Week**,
**Custom Hours**. The first two show one opening/closing time for all open days;
per-day rows appear only under Custom Hours. Stored values are unchanged.

## 8. Soft UI (neumorphism)

Shared styles in `src/theme/neumorphism.ts`: `neu.raised`, `neu.raisedSm`,
`neu.inset`, `neuPressable()`, `neuAccent()` and the bar shadows. Light falls from
the top-left everywhere.

- **Customer app:** Home, Search, Saved, Activity, Account, Notifications,
  business page, reviews, booking flow, login/sign-up, privacy, loading/error
  states and the tab bar.
- **Merchant:** header, tab bar, onboarding steps 1–3 and the submitted screen,
  Profile (including Store visits), Sales, Bookings and the Services editor.
  Looks and Inbox were left as they were.
- Kept flat or high-contrast on purpose: amounts and totals, status colours,
  charts, warnings and banners, and anything on top of photos or maps.
- Requires React Native's New Architecture (`boxShadow` with inset shadows).

## 9. Navigation and safe areas

- Customer tab bar: new Bootstrap icons for Search (`search-heart.svg`), Activity
  (`activity.svg`) and Account (`person.svg`), rendered and tinted through
  `expo-image` (no `react-native-svg`). Home keeps ⌂.
- The customer tab bar has an explicit height plus the system inset, so labels
  no longer run under Android's navigation bar.
- Services editor: the Save and Add Service bars now leave room for the
  navigation bar.

## 12. Known gaps

- The onboarding logo card says **Required** but a logo is not enforced.
- Sales: a booking paid and also typed in as a transaction is counted twice;
  range-priced services count at their minimum; there is no view of previous
  weeks or months.
- Phone numbers: the format check is loose (any 9 digits after 0/254) and the
  merchant contact editor and backend do not validate at all.
- Admin panel: only a business's main category can be edited.
- No iOS testing: iPhone builds need an Apple Developer account (TestFlight).
